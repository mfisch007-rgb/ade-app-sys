PURPOSE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Platform Bible is the highest-level architectural document describing the
entire ADE ecosystem.

It defines what the ADE Platform is, why it exists, how it is organized, and
how every subsystem fits together into one governed digital ecosystem.

Every architecture document within the repository derives from this document.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PLATFORM DEFINITION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Alpha Aliph Digital Ecosystems (ADE) is an AI-native Digital Infrastructure
Platform designed to provide reusable operational capabilities for businesses,
organizations, institutions, governments, and future digital ecosystems.

ADE is not a single application.

ADE is an ecosystem of interoperable platforms built upon one governed kernel.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PLATFORM VISION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Build one intelligent platform capable of supporting many products without
duplicating architecture, business logic, governance, or engineering effort.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PLATFORM OBJECTIVES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Build reusable digital infrastructure.
• Enable AI-first operations.
• Preserve organizational knowledge.
• Standardize engineering.
• Simplify integration.
• Support unlimited product growth.
• Encourage modular expansion.
• Maintain governance across every layer.
