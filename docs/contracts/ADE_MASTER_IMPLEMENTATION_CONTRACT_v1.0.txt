# 11. ADE INTERNAL COMMUNICATIONS EXCHANGE — ADE-ICX

## 11.1 Status

ADE-ICX is now a required first-class platform subsystem.

It must exist before the full APEX AIOPS/AIBOS marketplace rollout.

## 11.2 Purpose

ADE-ICX is the internal staff communications and organizational coordination system for the ADE ecosystem.

It covers staff operating across:

- ADE Core
- AWBULI
- PROCARTA
- ADE Infrastructure
- ADE ecosystem/platform units
- future ADE products and operational subsidiaries/units

## 11.3 Automatic staff onboarding

When an eligible person is formally onboarded/employed into an ADE organizational unit:

1. ADE identity is created/linked.
2. Organizational unit is assigned.
3. Department/team is assigned.
4. Role is assigned.
5. Communication entitlements are provisioned automatically.
6. Presence becomes available through the central system.
7. The staff member becomes discoverable according to their visibility policy.
8. Audit records are created.

Deprovisioning must occur when employment/access ends.

No stale employee access may remain active indefinitely.
